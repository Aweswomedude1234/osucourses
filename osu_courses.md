## Chemistry & Biochemistry (Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry)

### BIOCHEM 2210 — Elements of Biochemistry
- **Credit Hours:** 4
- **Prereq:** Chem 1110 (102) or 1210 (122), or equiv. Not open to students with credit for 211 and 212.
- **Description:** A survey of biochemistry stressing the qualitative rather than the quantitative approach. Credit does not count toward a major in biochemistry.

### BIOCHEM 2900H — Early Experience in Research in Biochemistry: Seminar
- **Credit Hours:** 1
- **Prereq:** Honors standing, and Chem 1220 (123), 1620 (163), or 1920H (203H); or permission of instructor. Not open to students with credit for 1900H or 200H. Graded S/U.
- **Description:** Introduction to biochemical research through seminars and laboratory tours by faculty in the department. Biology 1113 (113) recommended but not required.

### BIOCHEM 2998H — Early Experience in Research in Biochemistry: Laboratory
- **Credit Hours:** 1-3
- **Prereq:** Honors standing, and 2900H; or permission of instructor. Not open to students with credit for 1998H or 201H. Graded S/U.
- **Description:** A laboratory sequel to Biochem 2900H to introduce undergraduates to biochemical research.

### BIOCHEM 4511 — Introduction to Biological Chemistry
- **Credit Hours:** 4
- **Prereq:** Chem 1220, 1250, 1620, or 1920H, and 2510, 2310, 2610, or 2910H and one semester of Biological Sciences.
- **Description:** An introductory course in biochemistry dealing with the molecular basis of structure, metabolism, genetic replication, transcription, and translation in plants, animals, and microorganisms.

### BIOCHEM 5613 — Biochemistry and Molecular Biology I
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** Chem 2510 (252), 2610, or 2910H. Prereq or concur: Chem 2520 (253), 2620, or 2920H, and one semester in Biological Sciences. Not open to students with credit for 613 or 4511 (511).
- **Description:** An introductory course in biochemistry and molecular biology developing in three semesters the molecular basis of structure and function of living cells.

### BIOCHEM 5614 — Biochemistry and Molecular Biology II
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** A grade of C- or above in 5613.
- **Description:** Continuation of Biochem 5613.

### BIOCHEM 5615 — Biochemistry and Molecular Biology III
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** A grade of C- or above in 5614.
- **Description:** Continuation of Biochem 5614.

### BIOCHEM 5621 — Biochemistry and Molecular Biology Laboratory
- **Credit Hours:** 4
- **Prereq:** A grade of C- or above in 4511 or 5613, or equiv.
- **Description:** Laboratory course covering the principles and application of basic lab techniques, protein purification, enzyme assays, and recombinant DNA technologies.

### BIOCHEM 5721 — Physical Biochemistry I
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** Biology 1113; Math 1152 (153), 1172, 1181H, or 2162 (162); and Physics 1201 (113) or 1251 (133). Prereq or concur: Chem 2520 (253), 2620, or 2920H. Not open to students with credit for 721.01 or Chem 4200 or 4300.
- **Description:** Introduction to physical chemistry with emphasis on biological applications; designed for students in the life sciences.

### BIOCHEM 5722 — Physical Biochemistry II
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** 5721, or Chem 4200 or 4300. Not open to students with credit for 721.02 and 721.03.
- **Description:** Continuation of Biochem 5721.

### CHEM 1101 — Chemistry and Society
- **Credit Hours:** 4
- **Prereq:** Math 1050, or an ACT math subscore of 22 or higher that is less than two years old, or satisfactory score on Ohio State Math Placement Test. Not open to students with credit for 1100, 1110, 1210, 1610, 1910H, or any Chem course that uses these courses as prerequisites.
- **Description:** The course will examine the dilemma that we face in our modern world of attempting to balance the great benefits of modern chemical sciences and technology with the risks that accompany those benefits, through the lens of chemicals that have changed the world around us. This course includes a laboratory experience.

### CHEM 1110 — Elementary Chemistry
- **Credit Hours:** 5
- **Prereq:** Math 1073, 1074, 1075 or above; Math Placement Level L, M, N, or R; or ACT Math subscore of 22 or higher that is less than 2 years old. Not open to students with credit for 1210, 1250, 1610, or 1910H.
- **Description:** Introductory chemistry for non-science majors, including dimensional analysis, atomic structure, bonding, chemical reactions, states of matter, solutions, chemical equilibrium, acids and bases, along with topics in organic and biological chemistry.

### CHEM 1206 — Foundations 1 of General Chemistry
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq or concur:** Math Placement Level L or M, Math 1120, 1130, 1131, 1140, 1148, 1150, or above. Not open to students with credit for 1210, 1220, 1610, 1620, 1910H, 1920H, 1250.
- **Description:** Chem 1206 is the first course in a two-course series, for science majors, covering units & measurement, atomic structure, electron configuration, periodic trends, bonding, and molecular structure. The chemistry content is covered in the same depth and rigor as in Chem 1210, and is about 1/2 of the Chem 1210 content. Additionally, metacognitive learning strategies are taught in the course.

### CHEM 1208 — Foundations 2 of General Chemistry
- **Credit Hours:** 4
- **Prereq:** Chem 1206, and a grade of C- or above in Math 1120, 1130, 1131, 1140, 1148, 1150, or above. Not open to students with credit for 1210, 1220, 1610, 1620, 1910H, 1920H, 1250.
- **Description:** CHEM 1208 is second course in a two-course series, for science majors, covering the mole, stoichiometry, chemical reactions, thermochemistry, gases, liquids, and solids, paired with metacognitive learning strategies. CHEM 1208 also includes a laboratory experience, that covers labs from both CHEM 1206 and CHEM 1208 content. Credit for CHEM 1206 and CHEM 1208 is equivalent to CHEM 1210.

### CHEM 1210 — General Chemistry I
- **Credit Hours:** 5
- **Prereq:** One unit of high school chemistry, and Math Placement Level L or M; or a grade of C- or above in Math 1120, 1130, 1131, 1148, 1150, or above. Not open to students with credit for 1220, 1620, 1920H, 1250, 1610, or 1910H.
- **Description:** First course for science majors, covering dimensional analysis, atomic structure, the mole, stoichiometry, chemical reactions, thermochemistry, electron configuration, bonding, molecular structure, gases, liquids, and solids.

### CHEM 1220 — General Chemistry II
- **Credit Hours:** 5
- **Prereq:** 1210, 1215, 1250, 1610, or 1910H, and Math Placement Level L or M, or a grade of C- or above in Math 1120, 1130, 1131, 1148, or 1150, or above; or credit for both Chem 1206 and 1208.
- **Description:** Continuation of 1210 for science majors, covering solutions, kinetics, chemical equilibrium, solubility and ionic equilibria, qualitative analysis, thermodynamics, electrochemistry, descriptive chemistry, coordination compounds, and nuclear chemistry.

### CHEM 1250 — General Chemistry for Engineers
- **Credit Hours:** 4
- **Prereq:** One unit of high school chemistry; and Math Placement Level L or M, or a grade of C- or above in Math 1120, 1130, 1131, 1148, or 1150, or above. Not open to students with credit for 1210, 1610, 1910H, 2310, 2510, 2610, or 2910H.
- **Description:** First course for engineering majors, covering dimensional analysis, atomic and molecular structure, the mole, stoichiometry, chemical reactions, states of matter, solutions, kinetics, equilibrium, acids and bases, thermodynamics, and electrochemistry.

### CHEM 1610 — General Chemistry for Majors I
- **Credit Hours:** 5
- **Prereq:** One unit of high school chemistry, and Math Placement Level L or M; or a grade of C- or above in Math 1120, 1130, 1131, 1148, or 1150, or above, and enrolled in Chemistry or Biochemistry major; or permission of department. Not open to students with credit for 1210, 1220, 1250, 1620, 1910H, or 1920H.

### CHEM 1620 — General Chemistry for Majors II
- **Credit Hours:** 5

Not open to students with credit for 1220, 1920H, 2310, 2510, 2610, or 2910H.

### CHEM 2310 — Introductory Organic Chemistry
- **Credit Hours:** 4
- **Prereq:** 1110, 1220 (122), 1250 (125), 1620, or 1920H. Not open to students with credit for 2510 (251), 2610, or 2910H.
- **Description:** A condensed presentation of organic chemistry organized by functional groups with an emphasis on practical applications.

### CHEM 2510 — Organic Chemistry I
- **Credit Hours:** 4
- **Prereq:** Chem 1208, or 1210, or 1250, or 1610 or 1910H; and Chem 1220, or 1620 or 1920H; and Math Placement Level L or M, or a grade of C- or above in Math 1120, 1130, 1131, 1148, 1150, or above. Not open to students with credit for Chem 2520, 2610, 2620, 2910H, or 2920H.
- **Description:** Introduction to structure, nomenclature, physical properties, preparation and reactions of alkanes, alkenes, conjugated dienes, alkynes, alcohols, ethers, epoxides, aldehydes, and ketones. Other topics include stereochemistry, conformations of molecules, acids, bases, and reaction mechanisms.

### CHEM 2520 — Organic Chemistry II
- **Credit Hours:** 4
- **Prereq:** Chem 1208, 1210, 1250, 1610, or 1910H, and Chem 1220, 1620, or 1920H, and Chem 2510, 2610, or 2910H, and Math Placement Level L or M, or a grade of C- or above in Math 1120, 1130, 1131, 1148, 1150, or above. Not open to students with credit for Chem 2620 or 2920H.

### CHEM 2540 — Organic Chemistry Laboratory I
- **Credit Hours:** 2

### CHEM 2550 — Organic Chemistry Laboratory II

### CHEM 3301 — Science and Process of Drug Development
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** Biology 1113.xx; Chem 1220, 1250, 1620 or 1920H. Not open to students with credit for PHR 3301. Cross-listed in PHR.
- **Description:** The course focus is on the science and public policy that govern drug development. The principles of chemistry and biochemistry are applied to research and development of drug therapeutics. The drug approval process of drug safety, toxicity, and clinical trials will focus on pharmaceutical industry challenges of drug affordability, safety, and drug development impact on global public health.

### CHEM 3510 — Inorganic Chemistry
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** 2520 (253), 2620 (253), or 2920H (253H), or permission of instructor. Not open to students with credit for 652.
- **Description:** Introduction to the principles of inorganic structure and bonding, including molecular symmetry, atomic structure, ionic bonding, coordination complexes, magnetic properties, thermodynamics and reactivity, bioinorganic chemistry, and nanoparticles.

### CHEM 4200 — Physical Chemistry for Chemical Science I
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** 2210, 2510, 2610, 2910H, or equivalent; and Physics 1201 or 1251; and Math 1152 or 1172. Not open to students with credit for 4300 or Biochem 5721.
- **Description:** This course covers quantum mechanics and spectroscopy, beginning with an overview of classical mechanics and the historical origins of quantum theory. Basic quantum mechanics problems including the particle in a box, rigid rotor, harmonic oscillator, and hydrogen atom will be discussed, leading to a description of chemical bonding and modern computational molecular orbital theory (Hartree-Fock).

### CHEM 5230 — Neurotransmitter Chemistry
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** 2520, 2620, or 2920H, and 2540; or Grad standing; or permission of instructor. (Recommended prereqs: Chem 2550, and Biochem 4511 or 5613.)
- **Description:** Come and explore the natural and unnatural organic molecules involved in neurotransmission. Through the study of synthetic strategies, mechanistic principles, and the structural requirements for biological activity, students will investigate the chemical world of endogenous molecules, pharmaceuticals, and drugs of abuse.

### CHEM 5240 — Introduction to Protein Modeling
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** 2310, 2510, 2610, or 2910H; or Grad standing; or permission of instructor. (Recommended prereq: Biochem 4511.)
- **Description:** This course provides a practical introduction to the theory and methods of molecular modeling and computational chemistry as it pertains to modeling large biological molecules such as proteins. Hands-on experience will be obtained by all attendees in doing molecular mechanics and modeling dynamic systems (molecular dynamics).

### CHEM 5420 — Spectroscopy of Organic Compounds
- **Credit Hours:** 1.5
- **Prereq:** 2520 (253), 2620 (253), or 2920H (253H), or equiv. Not open to students with credit for 632.
- **Description:** Exploration of the use of spectroscopic techniques for the determination of the structure of organic molecules, including UV/vis, IR, NMR, and MS.

### CHEM 5430 — Carbohydrate Chemistry
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** 2520 (253), 2620, or 2920H (253H), or equiv.
- **Description:** Introduction to synthesis, conformation, and biological importance of carbohydrates and oligosaccharides, including nomenclature, protecting groups, glycoside synthesis, biosynthesis and biology, and NMR methods.

### CHEM 5440 — Introduction to Computational Chemistry
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** 2520 (253), 2620 (253), or 2920H (253H), or equiv. Not open to students with credit for 644.
- **Description:** Introduction to fundamental concepts in computational chemistry, including molecular modeling, molecular dynamics, and semi-empirical and ab initio calculations.

### CHEM 5450 — Practical NMR Spectroscopy
- **Credit Hours:** 1
- **Prereq:** 5420, or permission of instructor.
- **Description:** This course focuses on the application of NMR Spectroscopy to the structure determination and dynamics of primarily synthetic organic and organometallic products. The practical aspects of acquiring optimized, high-quality data are still beneficial for analysis of these spectra and will be presented as lectures and demonstrations.

### CHEM 5520 — Nanochemistry
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** 1220 (123), 1620 (163), or 1920H (203H), and permission of instructor. Not open to students with credit for 611.
- **Description:** Introduction to fundamental concepts of nanoscience, exploring strategies for complex assemblies of molecules and developing computational techniques for the investigation of nanotech structures.

## Business Administration & Entrepreneurship (Fisher College of Business — Class Search, Spring 2027, Columbus Campus)

*Source: OSU PeopleSoft Class Search (courses.erppub.osu.edu), Subject: Business Administration (BUSADM), Term: Spring 2027, Campus: Columbus. This tool does not have a department course-catalog page, so data below reflects one representative class section per unique course number, extracted via each course's Class Detail page (Units + Enrollment Requirements/Description, which includes the prereq). Graduate-level companion course BUSADM 7191 (Professional Experience in Business, Academic Career: Graduate) is omitted per undergraduate-only scope. BUSADM 5797 is included despite showing Academic Career "Graduate" because its own prereq/description explicitly restricts it to undergraduate BSBA students. No dedicated "Entrepreneurship" subject code exists at OSU; entrepreneurship courses are embedded within Business Administration (e.g., BUSADM 3533, BUSADM 4510.01) and other departments.*

### BUSADM 1100 — College of Business Survey
- **Credit Hours:** 0
- **Prereq:** NFYS, or a transfer student in direct enrollment program in BUS; and permission of instructor or department. Not open to students with credit for any OSU survey course.
- **Description:** Academic requirements; policies procedures and resources; student rights and responsibilities; academic areas of specialization; careers in accounting and business administration.

### BUSADM 1200 — College of Business Survey II
- **Credit Hours:** 1
- **Prereq:** Fresh standing, or transfer student survey course.
- **Description:** Students will develop a comprehensive academic, professional/career, and personal plan for success that incorporates: self-knowledge and individual interests, exploring a specialization, a 4-year graduation plan, short term and long term goals for gaining leadership and work experience, and identifying opportunities to enhance the college experience.

### BUSADM 2191.02 — Professional Experience Field Study
- **Credit Hours:** 0.5
- **Prereq:** Admission to College of Business major program and specialization, and permission of instructor. Not open to students with more than 3.5 credit hours of 2191 or 2191.01. Repeatable to a maximum of 4 cr hrs. Graded S/U.
- **Description:** For students completing a session long internship requiring credit; internship is supervised by the employer, three assignments related to the internship are evaluated by the Director, Undergraduate Internship Services.

### BUSADM 2600 — Career Exploration
- **Credit Hours:** 1
- **Prereq:** Enrollment in College of Business. Graded S/U.
- **Description:** Through guided reflection, interactive workshops, and meaningful engagement with Fisher faculty, alumni, and business professionals, students will explore diverse industries, functional roles, and career pathways available to business graduates.

### BUSADM 2601 — Career Foundations
- **Credit Hours:** 1
- **Prereq:** Enrollment in the Fisher College of Business Major Program and Specialization. Repeatable to a maximum of 3 cr hrs or 4 completions.
- **Description:** Explore the mindsets, strategies, and professional skills that drive successful careers in business while broadening your understanding of different career pathways. Engage directly with industry professionals through meaningful, interactive experiences and focus on developing self-awareness, strong communication skills, and career readiness.

### BUSADM 2798 — Study Tour: International
- **Credit Hours:** 1.5
- **Prereq:** Permission of instructor. Not open to students with credit for 498.02. Repeatable to a maximum of 9 credit hours or 3 completions.
- **Description:** Designed to provide an orientation to students who will embark on an international experience and once there, will enable students across countries to learn from each other.

### BUSADM 3360 — Elevate 360: A Practical Guide to Career and Life Success
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** None listed.
- **Description:** Imagine if you could take a class that teaches you all the practical things you're going to learn after college, but before you leave college...that's the idea behind Impact 360: A Practical Guide to Career and Life Success. This course aims to help students get a jumpstart on their careers and lives after college by teaching them the practical skills for how to achieve holistic life success.

### BUSADM 3533 — Technology Entrepreneurship
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** BusMhr 3510; or permission of instructor.
- **Description:** Examine the innovative transformation of knowledge captured in scientific discoveries into conceptual, viable, commercial products and services.

### BUSADM 3642.02 — Introduction to Sustainability in Business II: Applications, Solutions and Project Experience
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** 3640.02, or permission of instructor. Not open to students with credit for BusMHR 504.03 or 694.13.
- **Description:** Field projects on sustainable business issues.

### BUSADM 3760 — Exploring Culture in International Environments
- **Credit Hours:** 1
- **Prereq:** Accepted into a Fisher International Exchange Program. Repeatable to a maximum of 2 cr hrs.
- **Description:** Through individual reflections, activities, and discussions, students participating in Fisher's International Student Exchange Program will be challenged to view knowledge and experiences from multiple perspectives.

### BUSADM 3890H — Honors Seminar
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** Admission to a Business Administration Honors Program. Repeatable to a maximum of 6 cr hrs or 4 completions.
- **Description:** Honors seminar for students enrolled in the Business Administration Honors Program.

### BUSADM 4189 — Advanced Field Study: Doing Business in a Global Region
- **Credit Hours:** 1
- **Prereq:** Admission to Fisher College of Business Global Internship Program, and permission of instructor. Repeatable to a maximum of 2 cr hrs. Graded S/U.
- **Description:** Required pre-departure component of Fisher College of Business Global Internship Program for Undergraduates.

### BUSADM 4191 — Professional Experience in Business
- **Credit Hours:** 0
- **Prereq:** Permission of the Course Coordinator. Repeatable to a maximum of 4 completions. Graded S/U.

### BUSADM 4510.01 — Entrepreneurship: Practicum
- **Credit Hours:** 3

## Business Administration: Operations & Business Analytics (BUSOBA — Fisher College of Business, Spring 2027, Columbus Campus)

*Source: OSU PeopleSoft Class Search, Subject: Bus Admin: Operat & Bus Analyt (BUSOBA). Graduate-level 6000+/7000+ BUSOBA courses (6221, 7193, 7204, 7223, 7235, 7244, 7246, 7247, 7248, 7256, 7257, 7258, 7261, etc.) omitted per undergraduate-only scope.*

### BUSOBA 2320 — Decision Sciences: Statistical Techniques
- **Credit Hours:** 0
- **Prereq:** Math 1131 or 1151, and Stat 1430, and CSE 1113 or 2111, and Econ 2001.01. Prereq or concur: Econ 2002.01.
- **Description:** Examination of the use of statistical techniques in managerial decision-making processes; statistical inference, simple and multiple regression, time series.

### BUSOBA 2321 — Business Analytics
- **Credit Hours:** 0
- **Prereq:** Math 1131 or 1151 or equiv., and CSE 1113 or 2111, and Econ 2001.01 or equiv., and Econ 2002.01 or equiv.
- **Description:** Examination of the use of business analytic models used in managerial decision making processes. Emphasis on formulation and interpretation of models; supported by spreadsheet based software.

### BUSOBA 3130 — Foundations of Operations Management
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** Math 1116, 1130, 1148, or equiv, and Econ 2001.01, or equiv. Not open to students with credit for 3230. Not open to students enrolled in the BSBA degree program.
- **Description:** Introduction to operations & supply chain management to improve organizations; specifically analyzing, controlling & improving resources & processes to increase productivity, generate value-added output & meet business strategic & tactical goals.

### BUSOBA 3230 — Introduction to Operations Management: Improving Competitiveness in Organizations
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** Econ 2001.01, and 2002.01, and Stat 1430, or equiv.; or Math 1151 or 1154, and enrollment in BSET program. Not open to students with credit for 3130, or to students enrolled in UExp or PreBSBA-PR.
- **Description:** Introduction to operations and supply chain management to improve manufacturing and service organizations; analyzing, controlling and improving resources and processes to increase productivity, generate value-added output and meet business goals.

### BUSOBA 3230H — Introduction to Operations Management: Improving Competitiveness in Organizations (Honors)
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** Honors standing in BusAdm Honors Cohort program. Not open to students with credit for 630H.
- **Description:** Introduction to operations and supply chain management to improve manufacturing and service organizations; analyzing, controlling and improving resources and processes to increase productivity, generate value-added output and meet business goals.

### BUSOBA 3243H — Digital Product Management and Innovation
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** Honors standing; and enrollment in the Integrated Business and Engineering (IBE) program; or permission of instructor. Not open to students with credit for 3230 or 3230H.
- **Description:** This course introduces the principles and practices of digital product management. This multidisciplinary field combines strategy, design, and technology to create and manage digital products such as mobile apps, websites, and software.

### BUSOBA 3331 — Tactics in Analytical Discovery
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** Econ 2001.01; and 2002.01, or equiv; and CSE 2111, or equiv; and Math 1131, or 1151 or equivalent.
- **Description:** Understand how organizations can successfully collect, organize, manipulate, use and present data.

### BUSOBA 3332 — Business Analytics: Application of Predictive Analytics to Business Data
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** Econ 2001.01 and 2002.01 or equiv; and Stat 3202, or BusOBA 2320 and 2321; and CSE 2111 or equiv.
- **Description:** Build and test predictive models that move from data to parameter estimation.

### BUSOBA 3333 — Business Analytics: Applied Prescriptive Analytics
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** Econ 2001.01 or AEDEcon 2001; and Econ 2002.01; and Stat 3202, or BusOBA 2320 and 2321; and CSE 2111.
- **Description:** Moving from estimating model parameters, to making data-informed business decisions.

### BUSOBA 4193 — Individual Studies
- **Credit Hours:** 1-6
- **Prereq:** Permission of instructor. Repeatable to a maximum of 12 cr hrs or 6 completions. Graded S/U.
- **Description:** Individual study projects in selected areas in Management Sciences.

### BUSOBA 4232 — Operations Planning and Control
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** 2320 (330), 2321 (331), 3230 (630), and AcctMIS 2200 (211), 2300 (212); and BusMHR 2291 or 2292 (BusADM 499.01). Not open to students with credit for 732.
- **Description:** Review of a Planning and Control System. Theoretical frameworks and utilization of techniques to execute strategic and tactical goals to increase productivity and effectiveness of forecasting, scheduling, and inventory and capacity resources.

### BUSOBA 4233 — Operations Management Analytics
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** 2320 (330), 2321 (331), 3230 (630), and AcctMIS 2200 (211), 2300 (212); and BusMHR 2291 or 2292 (BusADM 499.01). Not open to students with credit for 733.
- **Description:** A study of data and modeling tools used in operations management. Emphasis is spreadsheet and database applications.

### BUSOBA 4234 — Service Operations
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** 2320 (330), 2321 (331), 3230 (630), and AcctMIS 2200 (211), 2300 (212); and BusMHR 2291 or 2292 (BusADM 499.01).
- **Description:** Surveys a broad framework of key operations management challenges, while also focusing on a broad variety of different services via in-depth case studies.

### BUSOBA 4239 — Managing Process Improvement
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** 2320 (330), 2321 (331), 3230 (630), and AcctMIS 2200 (211), 2300 (212), and BusMHR 2291 or 2292 (BusADM 499.01). Not open to students with credit for 739.
- **Description:** Describes the basics of establishing and managing a Lean process in service, office and manufacturing operations. We will look at how we can add value in delivery of goods or services to the customer.

### BUSOBA 4240 — Management of Technology
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** 2320, 2321, and 3230; and AcctMIS 2200 and 2300; and BusMHR 2291 or 2292.
- **Description:** This course explores technology and innovation from an operational perspective, emphasizing their role in driving competitive advantage. Students will analyze strategies, processes, and digital tools that enhance innovation capability, while strengthening their skills in strategic thinking, problem solving, and collaboration for effective operational leadership.

### BUSOBA 4250 — Six Sigma Principles
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** 2320, 2321, 3230, and AcctMIS 2200, 2300, and BusMHR 2292; or 3230, ISE 2040, and Stat 3440 or equiv., and enrollment in BSET program.

### BUSOBA 4251 — Six Sigma Projects
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** 4250 (710). Not open to students with credit for 711.
- **Description:** Six Sigma Projects class.

### BUSOBA 4253 — Sustainable Operations
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** 2320, 2321, 3230, AcctMIS 2200, 2300, and BusMHR 2292.
- **Description:** There is increasing pressure on businesses to pay more attention to the environmental consequences of the raw materials they source, processes they deploy, and the products and services they offer. ### BUSFIN 1200 — Personal Finance
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** Not open to students with credit for 220, or enrolled in Accounting or Finance majors.

- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prereq:** AcctMIS 2000, Math 1116 or above, Econ 2001.01. Not open to students with credit for 3220. Not open to students enrolled in the BSBA program.
- **Description:** Develops skills related to Time Value of Money, Risk and Return with applications in business and personal finance.

### BUSFIN 3220 — Business Finance
- **Credit Hours:** 0
- **Prereq:** Econ 2001.01 (200), and 2002.01 (201), and AcctMIS 2200 (211). Prereq or concur: AcctMIS 2300. Not open to students with credit for 620, or to students enrolled in UUSS, UExp or PreBSBA-PR.

- **Prereq:** 3120; and AcctMIS 2000. Not open to students with credit for 4221. Not open to students enrolled in the BSBA program. Cross-listed in CSCFFS.
